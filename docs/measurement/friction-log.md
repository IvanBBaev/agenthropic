## How to record a day

One section per day, newest last. Keep it short — two minutes at the end of the day.

- **Questions I had** — what you wanted to know about your agents today, in your own words.
- **Answered by** — `agenthropic`, the rival dashboard (L2), the raw JSONL, or nothing.
- **Friction** — where it failed, was slow or misleading; the view and roughly how long.
- **Wanted an alert?** — `yes` / `no`. `yes` means you would have wanted to be told without
  looking (this is the KC-5 count).
- **No use today** — write that line; a day without use still keeps the streak if it is
  recorded, and says something about need.

## Template

```md
### Day N — YYYY-MM-DD

- Questions I had:
- Answered by:
- Friction:
- Wanted an alert? no
```
